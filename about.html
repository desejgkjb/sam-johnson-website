01

The Origin Story

In February 2011, a devastating earthquake struck Christchurch. Sam Johnson was a university student watching his city break apart while thousands of young people were being told to stay out of the way. He disagreed.

Eleven thousand student volunteers answered.

02

What Started in Christchurch Didn't Stay There

The model Sam built in Christchurch — trust young people, give them a real problem, get out of the way — turned out to be useful far beyond New Zealand.

From local streets to global stages.

03

Where Sam Is Today

Sam is the Projects Director at STILL, a family office venture founded by Hideaki Fukutake. Through STILL, he works on projects that amplify the fundamentals of human nature, with a focus on people, nature, arts, and culture.
