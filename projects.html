Flagship

Student Volunteer Army

2011 – present · Christchurch & nationwide

When the Christchurch earthquakes struck, Sam used Facebook to mobilise students into a coordinated volunteer force, clearing thousands of tonnes of liquefaction silt from the city's streets and homes. The SVA has since grown into a national movement with school, tertiary and community programmes that put young people at the heart of civic service.
