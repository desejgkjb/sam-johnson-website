About Sam

From a student with a shovel to a global voice for young leaders.

When the Christchurch earthquakes struck, Sam Johnson mobilised 11,000 students through social media to dig the city out — and the Student Volunteer Army was born. Since then his work has taken him from disaster zones in Japan, the Philippines, Vanuatu and Nepal to boardrooms and parliaments across the Pacific, Saudi Arabia and Brazil.
